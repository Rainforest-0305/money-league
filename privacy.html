게임 안의 랭킹과 강제청산 알림에 등장하는 210명의 트레이더는 실제 사람이 아니라 기기 안에서 생성되어 자동으로 매매하는 컴퓨터 캐릭터(봇)입니다. 봇의 이름 목록과 매매 로직은 앱에 포함되어 있으며, 랭킹 계산도 기기 안에서만 이루어집니다. 이용자의 닉네임, 자산, 수익률, 주문 내역은 어떤 경우에도 다른 사람에게 전송되거나 공개되지 않습니다.

본 게임은 데이터를 네트워크로 전송하지 않는 구조 자체를 안전성 확보 조치로 삼습니다. 전송 구간이 존재하지 않으므로 전송 중 유출 위험이 없고, 사업자 측에 보관되는 데이터가 없으므로 사업자 서버를 통한 유출 위험도 없습니다. 기기에 저장된 데이터는 해당 기기의 운영체제 및 앱 샌드박스 보호를 받습니다.

10. 아동의 개인정보 (개인정보보호법 제22조의2)

본 게임은 개인정보 처리에 대한 동의를 받지 않으며, 동의를 처리 근거로 삼는 절차를 두지 않습니다. 회원가입, 로그인, 마케팅 수신, 제3자 제공, 광고 개인화, 인앱결제가 모두 존재하지 않고, 게임 진행 정보는 게임 제공이라는 목적만을 위해 이용자의 기기 안에서만 처리됩니다.

개인정보보호법 제22조의2제1항은 “이 법에 따른 동의를 받아야 할 때에는” 법정대리인의 동의를 받도록 정하고 있습니다. 본 게임은 동의를 처리 근거로 삼지 않으므로 만 14세 미만 이용자에 대해서도 별도의 법정대리인 동의 절차를 두지 않으며, 같은 조 제3항에 따라 아동이 이해하기 쉬운 언어로 본 방침을 안내합니다.

이 판단은 서버 · 계정 · 공개 랭킹이 없는 현재의 정적 단독 빌드를 전제로 합니다. 향후 온라인 랭킹, 이용자 간 공개, 마케팅성 알림, 인앱결제, 제3자 제공 등 동의를 처리 근거로 하는 기능을 추가할 경우, 그 시점에 연령 확인 및 법정대리인 동의 절차(시행령 제17조의2)를 별도로 마련하고 본 방침을 갱신합니다.

Privacy Policy — Money League

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Money League (the “Game”) is a standalone game that runs without any server. All game logic and all saved data stay on your device, and the Game does not transmit your information anywhere. The publisher (Rimi) does not collect, store, or have access to your game data.

Summary
  • There is no backend server, no user database, and no login account.
  • Game progress is saved only in your device's local storage.
  • The Game makes no network requests (even the fonts are bundled in the app).
  • There are no in-app purchases and no analytics SDKs.
  • The 210 other traders in the game are not real people. They are computer-controlled characters (bots) generated on your device.

3. Information Disclosed to Other Users

None. The Game has no feature that exchanges data with other users.

The 210 traders that appear in the ranking and in the liquidation feed are not real people. They are computer-controlled characters (bots) generated on your device that trade automatically; their roster and trading logic ship inside the app, and the ranking is calculated entirely on your device. Your nickname, assets, returns, and order history are never transmitted to or disclosed to anyone.

4. Third-Party Processing and Disclosure

The Game does not disclose personal information to third parties and does not outsource any processing. There is no personal information to outsource.

The typeface used on screen (Pretendard, SIL Open Font License) is bundled inside the app, so no external CDN is contacted. The Game contains no analytics tools, crash reporters, or social login SDKs.

6. Overseas Transfer

The Game has no server of its own and transmits no data externally, so no overseas transfer of personal information occurs.

7. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

All of the Game's data resides on your device, so you can delete it yourself at any time and it takes effect immediately.

  • Reset your record: In the app, go to [Settings] → [Sign out]. Your nickname and account record are erased and the game returns to its initial state.
  • Delete everything: Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data removes every item listed in section 2 from your device.

Because the publisher holds no user information, there is no data held by the publisher for you to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing for. Questions about this policy may be sent to the contact in section 11.

8. Destruction of Personal Information

The publisher holds no personal information, so no separate destruction procedure is maintained. Data stored on your device is destroyed the moment you delete it using the methods in section 7.

9. Security Measures

The Game's security measure is its architecture: it does not transmit data over a network. Because no transmission occurs, there is no risk of interception in transit, and because the publisher stores no data, there is no risk of a breach on the publisher's side. Data stored on your device is protected by that device's operating system and application sandbox.

10. Children's Personal Information (PIPA Article 22-2)

The Game does not obtain consent for the processing of personal information and maintains no procedure that relies on consent as a legal basis. There is no sign-up, no login, no marketing communication, no third-party disclosure, no personalized advertising, and no in-app purchase. Game progress is processed solely for the purpose of providing the game, and only on the user's own device.

Article 22-2(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea requires consent from a legal representative “when consent under this Act is to be obtained.” Because the Game does not rely on consent as a legal basis, no separate legal representative consent procedure is maintained for users under the age of 14, and in accordance with Article 22-2(3) this policy is written in language that is easy for children to understand.

This assessment is based on the current standalone static build, which has no server, no account, and no public ranking. If features that rely on consent as a legal basis are added in the future — such as online rankings, disclosure between users, marketing notifications, in-app purchases, or third-party disclosure — an age verification and legal representative consent procedure (Enforcement Decree Article 17-2) will be established at that time and this policy will be updated.
